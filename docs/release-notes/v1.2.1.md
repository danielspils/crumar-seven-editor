Expansions you own are no longer listed twice.
If you had an expansion the app didn't know about, it showed up twice in Sounds — once as unrecognized, and once as something you could still buy. Both about the same sound. Thanks to Rich Olivieri for reporting it with a photo of his screen; I couldn't have found this one myself, because I catalogue everything I own.
The three that were missing are catalogued now: Venice Grand C5, Venice Grand CFX and Venice Upright K8. I bought them, installed them, and read the names straight off my Seven.
Sounds you already own are no longer counted as available. "I don't know what's in this" isn't the same as "you don't have it."
Search inside a backup works. It was searching the wrong list and finding nothing, even for words on the screen. Searching also clears itself when you click away, so you don't come back to a filtered list wondering where everything went.
Under the hood: every future Crumar expansion can now be catalogued without buying it — the sound name is in the README that comes with the download. I guessed all three names that way before installing and got them character for character.

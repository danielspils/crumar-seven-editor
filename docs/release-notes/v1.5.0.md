- The UI for sending a patch or a whole bank to your Seven has been greatly improved
- With your Seven disconnected, patches are no longer wrongly flagged as needing a sound you already own.
- Saving patches has been consolidated. One save button instead of several scattered ones, it saves what you're actually hearing rather than what the file says, and it takes you to the new patch when it's done.
- The sound engine and effects columns now dim when you move away from a patch (and before you load another patch). It makes it easier to understand what you're hearing on the Seven when navigating the software. It'll make sense once you take it for a spin.

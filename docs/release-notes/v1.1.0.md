About those fake demo patches (used while I was building this Seven11)—they're removed automagically on update. Sorry about that! Anything you edited, renamed, or used in a setlist of your own is kept. If you don't know what I'm referencing, don't worry about it. It's all solved and your patches are (as always) safe.

A patch your instrument can't play now says so in the list, instead of appearing to do nothing when you select it.

Your library survives an interrupted write. Every file is written atomically, so a crash or a power cut can no longer empty your setlists.

Duplicating a backup record is now labelled and sits on the row it belongs to.

Left and right arrows move between banks (this is cooler than it sounds).
